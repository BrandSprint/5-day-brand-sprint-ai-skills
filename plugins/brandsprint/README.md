# BrandSprint AI Skills

The facilitator's thinking partner for the 5-Day BrandSprint. Run between sessions, never in the room.

Human creates. AI improves, tests, challenges, and guides the facilitator.

## What is inside

| Skill | When | What it does |
| :- | :- | :- |
| `sprint-board` | loaded by every other skill | The rule, the project folder, how the Miro board is read, the report format, the one way black stickies may be placed |
| `sprint-setup` | once, after Onboarding and the Discovery day | Interviews the facilitator, creates the project folder, tests the Discovery boards, returns candidate research questions |
| `sprint-research-prep` | after Research Preparation | Tests the Mindmap, methods, action plan and interview questions against the Discovery gaps; improves the questions |
| `sprint-research` | during the ten research days, and the evening before Day 1 | Extracts findings from the facilitator's own sources, offers them as SOURCE stickies, audits the four Evidence Walls |
| `sprint-day1` | evening of Day 1 | Stress-tests the locked Final Strategic Insight against the walls |
| `sprint-day2` | evening of Day 2 | Tests purpose, essence and the three statements against the insight and the walls |
| `sprint-day3` | evening of Day 3 | Tests values, archetype, sliders, tone and persona for coherence; returns a coherence map |
| `sprint-day4` | evening of Day 4 | Tests the prototype and the deck against the platform; may propose, labelled; rehearses Day 5 objections |
| `sprint-day5` | evening of Day 5 | Sorts the feedback into ACES as a proposal, re-tests the refined platform, checks the Brand Canvas against every lock |
| `sprint-handover` | the week after | Drafts the Brand Platform presentation, the Creative Brief, the board legend, next steps and the closing email, from locked content only |

## Requirements

A paid Claude plan, the Claude desktop app, Cowork, the Miro connector (bundled; sign in after install and choose the Miro team that holds the sprint board), and one folder on disk for sprint projects.

## Use

In Cowork: one chat per phase, the Sprints folder connected, the Miro connector on. Type `/brandsprint:sprint-setup` after the Discovery day, then the phase skill the evening of each session. Each skill writes its report into the project folder and asks before placing anything on the board.

The full guide is chapter 04 of The 5-Day Sprint guide.
