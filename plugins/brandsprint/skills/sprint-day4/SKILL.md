# Sprint · Day 4 · Brand Prototyping

Load `sprint-board` first; its rule, project folder, report format and placement rules apply in full. Open by reading `00 Project/project.md`, `00 Project/decisions.md` and the latest reports in `02 The Sprint/Day 2/` and `02 The Sprint/Day 3/`.

## When

The evening of Day 4, after the room assembled the deck. If the deck frame is empty, run the prototype tests and say the deck test could not run.

## What this skill is for, and the exception

Day 4 is prototyping, and the guide sets the floor, not the ceiling. This is the one day the AI may propose: concept directions the moodboard points to, references the moodboard lacks, angles for the announcement, a sharper beat for the storyboard. Every proposal is labelled "proposal" and goes in the report, never on a lock field and never on the board unless the facilitator asks for it. The tests still come first, because a prototype that does not answer the insight is a pretty thing with no parent.

Nothing in this skill writes the platform. Name, tagline, story and announcement are the room's; the AI may propose an angle for them, not the text, unless the facilitator asks for text explicitly, in which case the report marks it as AI-drafted and the room rewrites it in its own words.

## Frames to read

1. `Brand Strategic Direction`, `Brand Positioning Statement`, the Day 3 frames (values, archetype, sliders, tone, persona) as the platform to test against
2. `Brand Concept Moodboard`: read the images (fetch each image href and look at it) and the stickies
3. `Make Your Brand Alive`
4. `30s TV Ad Storyboard`
5. `Presentation Deck` or `Brand Platform Deck`, whichever the room used for the Day 4 deck
6. Any `Facilitator checks · …` frame

Report the inventory first, including how many images the moodboard holds per column.

## Canvas layouts

Brand Concept Moodboard: four quadrants. Top-left Messaging, top-right Logos & Marks, bottom-left Visual Identity, bottom-right Imagery & Illustration. References the group keeps coming back to carry a mark or a dot.

Make Your Brand Alive: left half, the words: Brand Name (top-left), Tagline (top, second column), Brand Story (left, tall), Brand Announcement (second column, tall: the launch post with image and caption). Right half, the references: Logo, Typography (top row), Colors, Imagery (bottom row).

30s TV Ad Storyboard: eight frames, two rows of four, read left to right, top to bottom, 1 to 8. Each frame: a sketch or image and one line under it: what we see, what we hear.

Deck: the template's slides in order: the ad, the announcement, a pause, the brand alive, then the reasoning. Grayscale by rule, so the brand's colours carry. Facilitation cues in speaker notes only.

## Tests, in order

### T1 · The moodboard
References per quadrant; an empty quadrant is a finding. Do the references come from research (brands the audience loves, worlds it lives in, per the Audience wall) or from the team's taste? For each marked reference, name the wall sticky or the persona trait it echoes, or "no link to the platform". The guide: ten references the group believes in beat forty that look nice; more than fifteen per quadrant with no marks is a reading.

### T2 · The brand alive, against the platform
Brand Name: is it one of the assets that can't change (project.md)? If so, it must be the existing one. Tagline: does it say the essence or the value statement in the audience's words, and is it a tagline rather than the essence written out? Brand Story: does it tell the insight (the truth about people), then the brand's answer? Brand Announcement: the guide's sharpest test, can the brand launch itself in one post? Does the post answer the insight, sound like the persona, sit on the tone positions? Quote the lines that do and the lines that don't. Logo, Typography, Colors, Imagery: against the Look & Feel positions (a Playful brand with a corporate serif, a Simple brand with a busy mark) and the archetype.

### T3 · The storyboard
Eight frames, in order, hook to logo. Does the story work without sound (read only the "what we see" lines)? Is the logo held to the last frame? Is there one sentence on what the ad has to leave behind, and do the eight frames leave that behind? Does the ad show the insight (the tension) before the brand's answer? Frames that show the product before the people are a reading.

### T4 · The deck
Order: ad, announcement, pause, brand alive, reasoning. A reasoning slide before the brand alive is a finding (show first, explain after). Any slide that names a tool, a day, or the method is a finding (the deck is about the brand, not the sprint). Grayscale, except the brand's own material. Cues on slides instead of in notes: finding. Does it run end to end as one story?

### T5 · Proposals, labelled
After the tests, and only after: up to three concept directions the moodboard and the platform point to, each in two sentences, each tied to the insight and the essence by name; references the moodboard lacks, by type (a messaging style, a mark, an imagery world), not by brand name unless the facilitator asks; one alternative angle for the announcement if the current one leaks the method or misses the insight; one sharper beat for the storyboard if the logo arrives early or the tension is missing. Every item begins with "Proposal:".

### T6 · Rehearsal for Day 5, for the facilitator only
For each Day 5 audience (customers one on one, employees as a group, key stakeholders as a group): three objections or misreadings the deck is likely to meet, written as the audience would say them, with the slide they attach to. Labelled "Rehearsal". These never go on the board, are never treated as feedback, and never enter the ACES Map.

## Output

Write the report in the `sprint-board` format to `02 The Sprint/Day 4/report-<date>.md`. Readings: the prototype against the platform, element by element. A section "Proposals" after Opinion, and a section "Rehearsal" at the end, each clearly headed. Next steps: what to fix in the deck before the first Day 5 session.

Closing question, exactly: "Place the top three findings on the board as black CHECK stickies in a frame 'Facilitator checks · Day 4'? Proposals stay in the report unless you say otherwise. yes / no / choose". Follow `sprint-board` for placement; proposals go on the board only on an explicit request, as black stickies prefixed "PROPOSAL ·", in the checks frame, never inside a Make Your Brand Alive field. Append the run to `decisions.md`.

## What this skill never does

Write the name, the tagline, the story or the announcement unless asked, and then only marked as AI-drafted. Generate images. Put a proposal inside a lock field or a Make Your Brand Alive field. Treat the rehearsal as evidence or feedback. Rewrite the deck.
