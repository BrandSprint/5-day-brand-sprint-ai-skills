# Sprint · Research Preparation

Load `sprint-board` first; its rule, project folder, report format and placement rules apply in full. Open by reading `00 Project/project.md`, `00 Project/decisions.md` and the latest report in `01 Before the Sprint/Discovery/`. The Discovery report's candidate research questions are the yardstick for this run.

## When

After the Research Preparation session, before the ten research days start. This is the one phase where the AI may improve content on the board's own terms, because the tools here produce a plan and a set of questions, not the platform. A rewritten interview question is still the facilitator's tool; it never becomes the brand's sentence.

## What this skill is for

Research is ten days of homework the team does alone, and a weak plan is impossible to fix on Day 1. The guide's rule for the phase: answer questions, don't confirm beliefs; desk and field, both; one finding per sticky with its source; 15 to 20 strong findings per wall. This skill checks whether the plan can deliver that, and sharpens the questions the team will carry into the field.

## Frames to read

1. `Stakeholder Mapping`
2. `Mindmap`
3. `Research method`
4. `Research Action Plan` (the room duplicates it, one per theme; read all frames whose title starts with it)
5. `Optional - Interview Questions - Customers` and `Optional - Interview Questions - Other Stakeholders`, if filled
6. The Discovery frames only when a test below needs a cross-check: `Competitor Landscape`, `Brand Perception`, `Research Questions` if it exists
7. Any `Facilitator checks · …` frame already present

Report the inventory first.

## Canvas layouts

Stakeholder Mapping: two overlapping rings. Left half (x under 0.45) is current customers, as specific groups. Right half (x over 0.55) is other stakeholders: employees, partners, distributors, media, regulators. Stickies in the overlap belong to both and are reported as such.

Mindmap: the brand in the centre (x 0.40 to 0.60, y 0.35 to 0.65). Five branches radiate outward: audience, competitors, market, trends, organization. The template does not fix the angle of each branch; read the branch label nearest to each cluster of stickies and group by it. Starred questions carry a star, a mark, or a second sticky.

Research method: two horizontal bands. Top band (y under 0.50) is desk research, bottom band (y over 0.50) is field research. Most items are the template's own pre-filled methods (shapes with text such as "News media", "Customer recordings"); the room's additions are stickies. Marked methods carry a mark or a dot sticky.

Research Action Plan: three stacked fields. Top (y under 0.30) Research topic. Middle (y 0.30 to 0.55) Research methods. Bottom (y over 0.55) Research plan: owner, deadline, needs.

Interview Questions (both): a left column (x under 0.25) describing the research group; a right area split into three bands: Introduction (y under 0.35), Topic (y 0.35 to 0.75), Closing (y over 0.75).

## Tests, in order

### T1 · Buyers and influencers
Stakeholder Mapping: is anything written as "everyone", "the public", "B2B", or another non-group? Each is a finding (the guide: specific groups, not everyone). Stakeholders mixed into the customer half, or customers in the stakeholder half, quoted. Are the groups research has to reach marked? Do those marked groups reappear as interview targets on the Interview Questions boards or in a Research Action Plan? Unmarked groups that Discovery's Brand Perception or Competitor Landscape named are readings.

### T2 · The Mindmap against Discovery
Every candidate research question from the Discovery report should sit on the Mindmap, under a branch, as a question. List the ones that are missing, by source board. List Mindmap items that are statements or topics rather than questions ("pricing" instead of "what do customers compare our price to?"). Count per branch; a branch with nothing under it is a finding. Starred questions: are there any, and would the starred ones change the strategy if the answer surprised the room? Name a question that would and is not starred, as a reading.

### T3 · Methods against questions
For each starred question, which method on the Research method board answers it? A starred question with no method is a finding. If every marked method sits in the desk band, that is a finding by the guide's own rule. Methods specific to the category that the room added under Other: list them; if none, a reading naming the kind of field method the category invites (a trade fair, the forum customers use, a competitor's shop), without inventing facts about the client.

### T4 · The Action Plan
One frame per theme: count them against the starred questions' themes. Per frame: topic filled; at least one method, from the shortlist; an owner by name; a deadline inside the ten days; what it needs. Any field empty is a finding, severity blocks for a missing owner or deadline. Themes that lean on desk research only, flagged. The Day 5 sessions: are they booked (customers one by one, employees as a focus group, key stakeholders as a group)? The guide asks for it here; if nothing on the board or in project.md says so, finding, severity weakens.

### T5 · Interview questions
If filled, for each board: the 3-5-3 structure (three introduction, five topic, three closing). Each topic question tested: open, not closed (answerable with yes or no); non-leading (does not contain its own answer); about real past behaviour, not opinions about the future; one question, not two in one. Closing questions on values and recommendations. The stakeholder board must not reuse the customer questions (the guide: a different angle). For each question that fails, write the improved version next to it in the report, keeping the room's intent and words where possible. This is the improvement this skill is allowed to make.

### T6 · Consistency
The themes on the Action Plan frames, the branches on the Mindmap, the interview topics, and the four Evidence Walls to come (Audience, Competitor, Market & Trends, Organization & Brand): will every wall receive findings from at least one planned method? A wall with no planned source is a finding, severity blocks, because Day 1 will feel it.

## Output

Write the report in the `sprint-board` format to `01 Before the Sprint/Research Prep/report-<date>.md`. In Readings, include the improved interview questions as a list, original and improved side by side, and the wall-by-wall coverage (which methods feed which wall). Next steps: what to fix before the first research day starts, in the order the room should take them.

Closing question, exactly: "Place the top three on the board as black CHECK stickies in a frame 'Facilitator checks · Research Prep', and the improved questions as black stickies next to the originals on the Interview Questions boards? yes / no / choose". Follow `sprint-board` for placement; improved questions go beside the original they replace, never over it. Append the run to `decisions.md`.

## What this skill never does

Research the client, the market or the competitors on the web; that is the Research phase, from the facilitator's sources. Decide which stakeholder group matters most. Write a research finding. Fill an empty Action Plan field with a guess.
