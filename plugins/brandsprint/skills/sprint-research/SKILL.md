# Sprint · Research

Load `sprint-board` first; its rule, project folder, report format and placement rules apply in full. Open by reading `00 Project/project.md`, `00 Project/decisions.md`, the latest report in `01 Before the Sprint/Research Prep/`, and whatever already sits in `01 Before the Sprint/Research/sources/`.

## When

Twice, usually. Halfway through the ten research days, when a thin wall is still easy to fix. And the evening before Day 1, as the final audit. Run it whenever the facilitator has new sources.

## What this skill is for

Two jobs. First, evidence the team gathered and never wrote down: an interview transcript holds thirty findings and the interviewer puts six on the wall. This skill reads the sources the facilitator hands over and offers the rest, each with its source, as black SOURCE stickies. The facilitator decides what goes up; the room never sees an unattributed sticky. Second, the wall audit the guide asks for: 15 to 20 strong findings per wall, every sticky with a source, evidence and assumptions in their own colours, no duplicates, and the six things on the slide 53 checklist.

The one hard rule: nothing lands on a wall without a source the facilitator has himself. A finding from a transcript names the transcript and the place in it. A finding from a document names the document and the page. General knowledge is not a source and never becomes a sticky.

## Step 1 · Sources

Ask first, in one message: which sources exist since the last run. Interview transcripts or recordings (customers, employees, stakeholders), photos of paper canvases or notes, survey exports, documents the client supplied, pages or reports the team read, links. Ask the facilitator to drop the files into `01 Before the Sprint/Research/sources/` or attach them. For a recording, ask for the transcript; if none exists, say that transcription is the facilitator's step and continue with what is there.

List every source read, with a short identifier the stickies will use (`int-04 customer`, `doc-brandbook-2024`, `photo-wall-audience-03`).

## Step 2 · Read the walls

Frames: `Evidence Wall - Audience`, `Evidence Wall - Competitors`, `Evidence Wall - Market & Trends`, `Evidence Wall - Organization & Brand`. Plus `Mindmap` and the `Research Action Plan` frames for the questions the research was meant to answer. Plus any `Facilitator checks · …` frame.

Layout: each wall is one open frame; stickies sit in a grid, left to right, top to bottom, with no zones. The template places twenty empty stickies on each wall; count empties separately. A photo of a paper wall placed on the frame is an image; read it when the facilitator says the wall was run on paper.

Report the inventory first: per wall, evidence count, assumption count, unlabelled count, empties.

## Step 3 · Extract from sources

For each source, list the findings it holds that answer a Mindmap question and are not already on a wall (match loosely against existing sticky text). Each finding: one sentence, in the source's own words where possible, the wall it belongs on, the source identifier and location (timestamp, page, question number). Mark each as EVIDENCE (heard or read in the source) and never as assumption; an assumption is the team's, not the source's.

Do not summarise a source into themes. Themes are Day 1's job. One finding per line, specific, as the guide wants it on the sticky: not "customers are unhappy" but what the customer said and about what.

Group the extracted findings by wall. If a wall receives more than twenty from sources, keep the twenty that answer starred questions and list the rest in an appendix of the report.

## Step 4 · Audit

### T1 · Wall strength
Per wall, against 15 to 20. Under 12 is a finding, severity blocks Day 1 if it is still under 12 the evening before Day 1, weakens at the halfway run. Assumptions outnumbering evidence on a wall: finding.

### T2 · Sources on stickies
Every evidence sticky should carry a source. List the ones with none. The guide's rule: no source makes it an assumption; recommend recolouring to the facilitator, never do it.

### T3 · Duplicates and near-duplicates
Pairs of stickies that say the same thing on the same wall, or the same finding on two walls. Quote both.

### T4 · Questions answered and not
Each starred Mindmap question: which stickies answer it, on which wall. Starred questions with no finding on any wall, after the sources are included, are findings, severity blocks the evening before Day 1. Findings that answer no Mindmap question are readings, not findings; they may still matter on Day 1.

### T5 · Walls against the plan
The Research Action Plan frames: which planned methods produced findings on the walls, which produced none. A method with an owner and a deadline and no findings is a finding at the halfway run.

### T6 · The checklist, the evening before Day 1
From the guide, six things: the Decider is named (project.md); every seat is booked for all five days (project.md); four walls at 15 to 20 with sources and no duplicates (T1 to T3); the room or the board ready (canvases loaded, two sticky colours, one wall for the locks, from the facilitator); Day 5 sessions booked (project.md or the Research Prep report); consent agreed (project.md). Report each as done, open, or unknown.

## Output

Write the report in the `sprint-board` format to `01 Before the Sprint/Research/report-<date>.md`. Section 4, Readings, holds the extracted findings grouped by wall, each with its source and location, and the answered-and-unanswered map of starred questions. Section 5, Opinion: can these walls carry Day 1, in two or three sentences, and which wall worries you. Section 6: what to do in the remaining research days, by owner where the plan names one.

Closing question, exactly: "Place the extracted findings on their Evidence Walls as black SOURCE stickies (text, then source and location), and the top three findings as black CHECK stickies in 'Facilitator checks · Research'? yes / no / choose". Follow `sprint-board` for placement: SOURCE stickies go in free space on the matching wall, never over an existing sticky. Report what was placed per wall. Append the run to `decisions.md`.

## What this skill never does

Put a fact on a wall that has no source in the facilitator's hands. Search the web for findings about the client, the market or the competitors. Interpret the walls into insights, tensions or themes; that is Day 1 and the room's. Recolour, move or merge existing stickies. Transcribe audio it cannot read.
