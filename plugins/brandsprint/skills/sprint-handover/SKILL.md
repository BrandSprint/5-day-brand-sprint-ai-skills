# Sprint · Handover

Load `sprint-board` first; its rule, project folder, report format and placement rules apply in full. Open by reading `00 Project/project.md`, `00 Project/decisions.md` and every report in `02 The Sprint/`, Day 5 last.

## When

The Monday and Tuesday after the sprint, as the guide's "two days after" page lays out: Monday the presentation and the brief, Tuesday the board and the next steps, then one email. The guide's reason for the pace: a week later the team forgets why it chose what it chose.

## What this skill is for, and the line it holds

This is the only skill that writes for the client. It writes up; it does not decide. Every sentence in every draft traces to a lock on the board or to a quote from the room, and each draft ends with the list of board items it was built from, so the facilitator can check the ancestry before anything is sent. Where a lock is missing or two frames disagree, the draft shows a gap in brackets and the report lists it as a finding; the skill never fills a gap with its own content.

The drafts quote the room. The guide's line for the brief: the builders should hear the sprint, not a summary of it.

## Frames to read

Every lock: `Final Strategic Insights`, `Brand Strategic Direction`, `Simplified Value Statement`, `Simplified Distinctiveness Statement`, `Brand Positioning Statement`, `Core Brand Values`, `Rewrite Brand Values`, `Brand Archetypes`, `Look & Feel Slider`, `Tone of voice`, `Brand as a Person`, `Make Your Brand Alive`, `30s TV Ad Storyboard`, `Brand Concept Moodboard`, `ACES Map`, `Brand Canvas`. The four Evidence Walls, for the evidence behind the insight. The Validation guide notes for the reactions. The whole board's frame list, for the legend. Any `Facilitator checks · …` frame, so they can be listed for removal.

If the sprint ran on paper and the board holds photos, say which frames are photos and ask the facilitator for the text of any lock the photo does not show legibly.

## Step 1 · Pre-flight

Before drafting: the Day 5 report's findings that block the handover, still open? List them and stop at the drafts' outline if any is open, unless the facilitator says to proceed. Every lock present and not "Write here"? The Brand Canvas consistent with the board (the Day 5 table)? Report the inventory and these checks first.

## Step 2 · The Brand Platform presentation

Six parts, in the guide's order, no tool names, no day labels, written for people who weren't in the room:

1. The insight: the Final Strategic Insight, and the evidence it stands on (three to five wall stickies, quoted, with their wall named as a source of research, not as a "wall").
2. The direction: purpose and essence, with the case for this essence from the Brand Strategic Direction frame.
3. The position: the value statement, the distinctiveness statement, the positioning statement.
4. The personality: values with their behaviour lines, archetype (primary, secondary), the brand as a person, look & feel positions, tone positions with the in-tone example.
5. The brand alive: name, tagline, story, the eight storyboard frames in words, the announcement post.
6. What we heard: the reactions from the reality check by audience, verbatim where the notes allow, and what the team changed because of them, from the ACES adopted list.

Format: a markdown document, one heading per part, the room's sentences verbatim under each, the facilitator's connective text kept to one or two sentences per part. Save as `03 After the Sprint/brand-platform/brand-platform-presentation-<date>.md`. If the facilitator wants slides, say that this document is the content and the slides are a separate build from it.

## Step 3 · The Creative Brief

For the people who build the expression: copywriters, designers, the naming team. Structure from the guide:

The core: insight, purpose, essence, positioning, in the platform's words. The verbal lane: tone positions, the in-tone and off-tone examples, custom attributes, the persona's voice, the messaging references from the moodboard with the reason the team chose them. The visual lane: look & feel positions with the three or four that are the brief, archetype, the logo, typography, colour and imagery references from the moodboard with the reasons. The job: deliverables (from the next steps, Step 5), mandatory elements (the assets that can't change, from project.md, with their reasons), who decides (the Decider and the owner per deliverable), and how drafts will be judged.

The judging criteria are written from the platform as tests, in the guide's spirit: a draft that misses goes back with the failed test named, not with adjectives. Each criterion is one sentence that points at a lock: "Does it answer the insight: [insight]?", "Could the closest competitor sign it?", "Does it sit where the sliders put it: [positions]?", "Is it in tone: compare with [in-tone example]?", "Does it hold the mandatory elements?".

Save as `03 After the Sprint/creative-brief/creative-brief-<date>.md`.

## Step 4 · The board legend and archive

From the frame list: a legend the client can read six months from now. What is where, in what order to read it, which frames hold locks, which hold the candidates that lost (kept, because the reason for a choice lives in what lost), which hold the raw research. A list of loose items and the parking lot to move into one archive frame. A list of every `Facilitator checks · …` frame and every black sticky, to be removed before the handover (the client's board carries no trace of the checks).

Save as `03 After the Sprint/board-legend.md`. Offer to place the legend on the board as a text item in a frame titled "Read me first", at the top-left of the board, on the facilitator's yes; this is the one write to the client's board this skill may make, and it is the facilitator's text once he approves it.

## Step 5 · Next steps and reopen triggers

Next steps: a table of what comes next in building the brand (naming if the name is open, visual identity, communication, website, the internal launch, whatever the room agreed in the wrap-up), with order, owner and decider per step. Only what the room or the facilitator said; empty cells stay empty and are listed as findings.

Reopen triggers, from the guide's living-platform page: a new competitor or a price war, a shift in who buys or why, a new product, market or merger, a trend that changes the category, and a regular check (once a year as the default). For each trigger, which decision it touches and which tool to rerun, from the reopening page: name the change, find the decision it touches, rerun that tool and everything downstream, check the insight last, log it.

Save as `03 After the Sprint/next-steps.md`.

## Step 6 · The closing email

One email, in the facilitator's voice, to the client: the four wrap-up answers (what we made, what we heard, what changed, what comes next) in a short paragraph each, then the list of what is attached or linked (the Brand Platform presentation, the Brand Canvas as a PDF, the Creative Brief, the board link, the next steps), then when the platform should be reopened, then thanks. No tool names, no day labels. The email closes the project, in the guide's words.

Save as `03 After the Sprint/handover-email.md`.

## Output

Write the report in the `sprint-board` format to `03 After the Sprint/report-<date>.md`: the inventory and pre-flight, the findings (every gap in brackets in any draft is a finding, with the frame that should have held it), the list of drafts written with their paths, and for each draft the list of board items it was built from. Opinion: what in the platform you would expect the builders to push on first. Next steps: what the facilitator reviews before sending, in order.

Closing question, exactly: "Place the board legend on the board in a 'Read me first' frame? yes / no. And confirm: remove the Facilitator checks frames and black stickies before the handover? I will list them; you delete." Follow `sprint-board`: the legend is the only write, and only on yes. Append the run to `decisions.md`.

## What this skill never does

Fill a gap in a lock with its own content. Rewrite a lock to read better; it quotes. Decide a next step, an owner or a decider. Delete anything on the board. Send the email. Produce slides or a PDF without being asked, and then only from the markdown it wrote.
