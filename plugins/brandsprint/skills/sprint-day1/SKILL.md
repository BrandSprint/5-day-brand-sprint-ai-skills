# Sprint · Day 1 · Sense-making

Load `sprint-board` first; its rule, project folder, report format and placement rules apply in full. Open by reading `00 Project/project.md`, `00 Project/decisions.md` and the latest report in `01 Before the Sprint/Research/`.

## When

The evening of Day 1, after the room locked the Final Strategic Insight. If the Final Strategic Insights frame is empty or still says "Write here", stop after the frame inventory and tell the facilitator the day has not locked; there is nothing to test yet.

## What this skill is for

Day 1 goes from 60 to 80 stickies to one sentence, and the room locks it at the end of a long day. The tests below are the ones the guide already asks the room to run. You run them again, slowly, with the walls open, and report where the sentence does not hold. You do not write a better sentence, not as a suggestion, not as an example.

## Frames to read

Through `sprint-board`, in this order:

1. `Evidence Wall - Audience`, `Evidence Wall - Competitors`, `Evidence Wall - Market & Trends`, `Evidence Wall - Organization & Brand`. These are the pointing surface for every test. Keep the full item lists.
2. `Data Cluster - Audience`, `Data Clusters - Competitors`, `Data Clusters - Market & Trends`, `Data Clusters - Organization & Brand`.
3. `Facts & Truths`.
4. Every frame titled `Insight Rewrite` (the room duplicates it, one per participant; read all).
5. `Strategic Insights Map`.
6. `Final Strategic Insights`.
7. If filled: `Optional - Customer Braindump`, `Optional - Main Customer Profile`, `Optional - Competitor Braindump`, `Optional - Main Competitor Profile`. Read them only as extra pointing surface.
8. Any `Facilitator checks · …` frame already present.

Report the inventory first, one line per frame: item count, empty stickies, and "missing" for frames not found.

## Canvas layouts (the images carry no text; this is how to read positions)

Positions are fractions of the frame, from `sprint-board`.

Data Cluster frames: four boxes in a 2 by 2 grid. In each box, the left 70 percent is the cluster (grouped wall stickies and a cluster name), the right 30 percent is the Facts column, where the one-sentence facts and truths pulled out of that cluster sit.

Facts & Truths: a 2 by 2 matrix. Top-left Audience, top-right Competitors, bottom-left Organization & Brand, bottom-right Market & Trends. Lines or connectors between two stickies mark a tension the room drew.

Insight Rewrite: three stacked fields, at roughly y 0.30, 0.56 and 0.83. Each field is one candidate insight. A field that still reads "Write here" is unused.

Strategic Insights Map: the left 30 percent is the candidates column. The middle holds three filter boxes stacked top to bottom: Relevance, Differentiation, Sustainability. The right 40 percent is where survivors go. A candidate sticky sitting in the right zone passed all three filters in the room's judgement; a sticky left in the candidates column or beside a filter box did not.

Final Strategic Insights: the top half is "Selected insights", the survivors side by side. The bottom box is "The final strategic insight", one sentence. A sticky or text under or beside it is the reason for the choice.

## Tests, in order

Run each test and record its result before moving to the next. A test that cannot run (frame missing, field empty) is reported as "not testable" with the reason, never skipped silently.

### T1 · Wall strength
Count evidence and assumption stickies per wall. The guide asks for 15 to 20 findings per wall. A wall under 12, or a wall where assumptions outnumber evidence, is a finding, because every later test points at these walls and a thin wall cannot carry an insight. Severity: weakens Day 2.

### T2 · Truth specificity
For every sticky in a Facts column of the Data Cluster frames: is it one sentence, and is it specific enough to carry a strategy? The guide's own contrast: not "customers are unhappy" but "customers choose us for delivery speed, then stay for the after-sale communication". Quote each generic truth as a finding. Also list Facts stickies with no cluster stickies beside them (a truth with no wall behind it).

### T3 · Matrix and tensions
Facts & Truths should hold three to five truths per quadrant, each traceable to a Facts sticky on a Data Cluster frame (match text loosely). Report quadrants over five or under three, and truths that appear on the matrix but on no cluster board. Then read across the quadrants as the guide says, not down them: list every pair of truths from different quadrants that pull against each other and that no line connects. Quote both stickies. This is pointing at what is already on the board, not proposing content.

### T4 · Candidate format and the three negative tests
For every filled candidate on every Insight Rewrite frame:
- Format: does it read "[something true about the audience or the world] which means [what it means for the brand]"? A candidate with no "which means" clause, or one whose first half is about the brand rather than about people or the world, fails.
- Origin: does its first half trace to a tension on the matrix or a truth in a quadrant? Name the truth or write "no matching truth on the matrix".
- The three negative tests from the guide: is it specific enough to drive one action; would the average person in the category already say it; could a competitor sign the same sentence. Answer each in one line with the reason. A yes to the second or third means observation or cliché, in the guide's words.
Report failures per candidate. Do not rank candidates against each other and do not say which one you would have chosen.

### T5 · The filters, with evidence
For every sticky in the survivors zone of the Strategic Insights Map, and for the ones the room left beside a filter box:
- Relevance: which wall stickies show this matters to the customer. Quote them, or "no evidence found on the walls".
- Differentiation: which wall stickies show it sets this brand apart, and which Competitors wall stickies show a competitor already claims the same. Quote both sides.
- Sustainability: which wall stickies show the brand can own it long term (Organization & Brand wall).
A survivor with no evidence on any one filter is a finding: "passed in the room without evidence on [filter]". Severity: blocks Day 2 if it is the one that became the final insight, weakens it otherwise.
If more than three stickies sit in the survivors zone, report it as the guide does: the filters were too soft.

### T6 · The locked sentence, word by word
Take the sentence in "The final strategic insight" exactly as written. Split it into its claims (usually three to five: the truth about people, the qualifier, the "which means", the job for the brand). For each claim, find the wall stickies behind it, quoting them with their wall. Then:
- Ancestry: list the words or claims with no sticky behind them. The guide's rule: the part with no evidence is opinion, and it goes. Report each as a finding with severity "blocks Day 2" if it is the "which means" half, "weakens" otherwise.
- More than one wall: name the walls the sentence rests on. One wall only is a finding (one beloved quote is not an insight).
- Competitor signature: could the closest competitor on the Competitors wall sign this sentence? Answer with the competitor sticky that makes it possible, or "no".
- Action: name the one action the sentence drives for Day 2. If you cannot name one, that is the finding.
- Drift: compare the locked sentence with the survivor it came from. Any claim in the final sentence that appears in no survivor and no wall sticky was added at the lock. Quote it.
- The reason: is there a reason for the choice written under the sentence, as the guide asks. If not, finding, severity worth knowing.

### T7 · Optional tools, if filled
If the Main Customer Profile or Main Competitor Profile is filled: does the locked insight speak to the main customer group the room chose, and does it sit on the open flank the room circled on the competitor profile. Report mismatches as "worth knowing".

## Output

Write the report in the `sprint-board` format to `02 The Sprint/Day 1/report-<date>.md`. In it: the frame inventory; one line, "Nothing blocks Day 2." or "N findings block Day 2."; the findings ranked by severity with the top three marked; as Readings, the locked sentence annotated claim by claim with the wall stickies behind each or "no evidence found on the walls", plus the tensions on the matrix no candidate used and the walls the lock does not touch; Opinion, short, labelled; next steps for the facilitator before the Day 2 morning check.

Then the closing question, exactly: "Place the top three on the board as black CHECK stickies in a frame 'Facilitator checks · Day 1'? yes / no / choose". Follow `sprint-board` for placement. Append the run to `decisions.md`.

## What this skill never does

Propose an insight, an alternative wording, or a "stronger version". Rank the room's candidates. Mark a filter as passed on its own judgement; only the walls pass a filter. Supply a fact from outside the board. Write to the board before the closing question is answered.
